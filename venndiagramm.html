Publications & Design Venn Diagramm

Venn Diagramm is a small-run zining and publishing imprint. Maddy Angstreich and I co-founded Venn Di to build ourselves a space for experimentation and play (though we sometimes accept commissions).

Venn Diagramm has exhibited at art fairs around the world, including Press Play, Staple & Stitch, and Glue. Our work has been featured in New York Magazine, among others. Some of our publications are free to read, print, and share, and many are available in hardcopy via our stockists. For updates, visit our website or follow us on Instagram.
