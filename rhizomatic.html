Identity & Posters Rhizomatic

Visual identity for Rhizomatic, a craft cafe and community space in Hackney, London. Inspired by Art Nouveau — a movement rooted in the belief that art and craft are inextricable from the world around them — Rhizomatic's design system evokes the organic entanglement of its namesake, the rhizome.
