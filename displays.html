Display Systems design & fabrication

Set of aluminum shelves, designed to flexibly scaffold printed matter. Each worlds a range of builds and interactions, and suggests a broader sense of what publications could be.

System 01 accommodates printed matter in multiple orientations. Although relatively small and portable, its open build can home printed matter at a wide range of sizes.

System 02 is a modular, multipurpose framework: it can lay flat, lean, or wall-mount, and its shelves can be moved and removed to reapportion space.
