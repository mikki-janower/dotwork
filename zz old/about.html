I'm Mikki, a designer and anti-disciplinarian. I'm currently an Art Director at Bloomberg Businessweek. Previously, I worked on news design and art direction at The New York Times, and built microsites, brands, and special projects on Squarespace's brand team. I've partnered with agencies like Base, Mother, and Morcos Key, and worked with clients like the Wall Street Journal and the Guggenheim Museum. I co-founded and co-operate Venn Diagramm Press.
